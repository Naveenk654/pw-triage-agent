# Privacy and Data Policy

## 1. Data Collected
We collect personal information including name, phone number, email address, academic details, device information, and payment records to provide and improve our services.

## 2. How Data is Used
Your data is used for service delivery, platform personalization, analytics, and sending relevant promotional communications about new batches and offers.

## 3. Opting Out
Users can opt out of marketing and promotional emails or SMS notifications by updating their communication preferences in the PW app settings.

## 4. Account Deletion
To request permanent deletion of your account and personal data, please email support@pw.live using your registered email address and phone number. Deletion requests are processed within 30 days.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
