# Vidyapeeth Offline Centers Policy

## 1. Admission Process
Students can enroll in PW Vidyapeeth centers via two methods:
- **Online:** Register and pay fees through pw.live.
- **In-Person:** Visit the nearest center between 9:00 AM and 8:00 PM for counseling and enrollment. 
For counselor support, call 07019243492.

## 2. Fee Structure
The fee structure varies by city, specific center, and course (JEE/NEET/Foundation). An initial registration or seat booking fee of approximately **₹5,000** is typically required and is non-refundable.

## 3. Hostel and Residential Facilities
Select cities (e.g., Kota, Patna, Varanasi, Noida, Bangalore) offer the Vidyapeeth Residential Program. Facilities include:
- 24/7 CCTV and security
- Wi-Fi and nutritious meals
- Live-in mentors and supervised study hours

## 4. Center Transfer Policy
Transfers are only permitted between centers of the same type (e.g., from one Vidyapeeth to another). Transfers from Pathshala (hybrid) to Vidyapeeth (pure offline) are not allowed.

## 5. Offline Refund Rules
The registration/seat booking fee is strictly non-refundable. If a refund is requested before the commencement of classes, a partial refund of the tuition fee may be processed. Requests made after 7–10 days from the batch start date are not entertained.

## FAQ
**Q: Can I visit the center before enrolling?**
A: Yes, centers are open daily from 9 AM to 8 PM for visits and counseling.

**Q: Is the hostel compulsory for Vidyapeeth students?**
A: No, hostel enrollment is optional and subject to availability.
