# EMI and Installment Policy

## 1. EMI Availability
PhysicsWallah offers no-cost EMI options through our partnered lending platforms (e.g., Cardless credit, Credit Card EMI) to make course payments flexible.

## 2. Grace Period
If an EMI payment is missed, the system provides a 5-day grace notification period. You will receive SMS and email reminders to clear the pending dues.

## 3. Batch Suspension
If the EMI is not paid within the 5-day grace period, all batch privileges and app access related to that purchase will be automatically suspended.

## 4. Re-activation
Upon successful payment of the pending EMI installment, batch access is re-activated within **6 hours** of settlement verification by the lending partner.

## 5. Lending Partner Terms
All EMI agreements are governed strictly by the respective lending partner's terms and conditions. PW is not liable for credit score impacts, late fees charged by the lender, or disputes arising from the lending agreement.

## FAQ
**Q: My EMI bounced and my batch is locked, when will it reactivate?**
A: Once you clear the due amount with the lending partner, access will be restored within 6 hours.

**Q: Can I switch from EMI to full payment midway?**
A: Yes, you can foreclose the loan directly through the lending partner's app or portal.
