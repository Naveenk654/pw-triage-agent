# Policy: Examination and All India Test Series (AITS)
Last updated: 2026-09-11

## 1. Test Window Schedules and Live Ranking
All India Test Series (AITS) mock exams open at 09:00 IST on designated Sundays and close at 21:00 IST on the same evening. Students who submit tests within this 12-hour synchronized window are eligible for National Percentile and All India Rank (AIR) computation. Submissions received past 21:00 IST will generate marks and solution reports but are excluded from official rank rosters.

## 2. Test Attempt Limits and Retake Policies
Every scheduled mock exam permits exactly one official live attempt for scoring and ranking. Once the test window concludes, the examination converts into unrestricted practice mode, allowing the student to review correct options, read text explanations, watch video solutions, and retake questions without altering their historical leaderboard score.

## 3. Technical Malfunctions During Live Tests
Should an unexpected local device freeze or transient internet interruption take place during an ongoing test, the examination platform preserves answers chosen up to the latest heartbeat packet. Students may resume the test from any authorized device within the open window; however, elapsed test clock timers do not pause during disconnection.

## 4. Unfair Means and Anti-Cheating Penalties
Copying, switching browser tabs during fullscreen proctored exams, or running unauthorized background applications will cause the test proctoring engine to log a disciplinary flag. Accumulating 3 disciplinary flags leads to instant auto-submission with a zero score and disqualification from scholarship evaluations.

## 5. Common Questions
- Can I take the Sunday test on Monday morning? Yes, but you will only receive practice mode analytics and no All India Rank.
- Can I retake a test to improve my test rank? No, official ranks depend solely on the initial synchronized attempt.
