# Policy: Account Management and Security Policy
Last updated: 2026-09-02

## 1. Registered Mobile Number and OTP Authentication
Every student account is tied to a single primary 10-digit Indian mobile phone number, which acts as the mandatory username and OTP verification recipient. Requests to alter the primary registered mobile number require submission of government photo ID (Aadhaar or School ID) and parental consent through our secure verification form, taking 48 to 72 hours to finalize.

## 2. Device Limit and Concurrent Session Controls
To safeguard digital copyright, each account permits an active login on a maximum of two devices simultaneously (for example, one smartphone and one desktop/laptop browser). Logging in on a third hardware device automatically triggers an invalidation token that logs out the oldest existing session without warning.

## 3. Account Suspension for Terms Violations
Accounts caught utilizing video ripping software, automated scraping bots, credential sharing on public Telegram groups, or posting abusive language in the live lecture chat will be subject to immediate temporary suspension (7 days) or permanent administrative ban upon repeated infringement, without entitlement to fee rebate.

## 4. Inactive Accounts and Data Retention
Accounts that register zero logins for over 18 consecutive months are placed into dormant status. Student academic transcripts and payment receipts are retained for 3 years in compliance with statutory audit norms. Dormant profiles can be reactivated by initiating password recovery via registered OTP.

## 5. Common Questions
- Can I use my account on both my phone and my sister's tablet? Up to two active devices are permitted concurrently on a single profile.
- I lost my SIM card, how do I login? Contact student support with identity documentation for registered phone number reassignment.
