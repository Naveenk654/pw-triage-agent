# Policy: Escalation Contacts, Grievances, and Tier-2 Support
Last updated: 2026-09-16

## 1. Standard Support Channels and Primary Response SLAs
For general queries regarding course schedules, portal features, or receipt generation, students must initially raise a ticket through the in-app Help & Support desk or email support@pw-fictional.edu. First-response acknowledgment is provided within 12 hours, with full resolution targets of 24 to 48 hours for standard tier inquiries.

## 2. Escalation Tier Matrix and Priority Criteria
If a ticket remains unaddressed after 48 hours, or in situations involving payment disputes, legal consumer complaints, recurring access lockouts, or severe distress, the issue qualifies for Tier-2 supervisory escalation. Urgent priority handling is reserved for verified transaction failures exceeding 72 hours, batch lockout before an exam within 24 hours, or urgent fee disputes.

## 4. Conduct Guidelines and Malicious Claims
Support channels are maintained to assist learners professionally. Threats of frivolous litigation, abusive communication toward customer service executives, or fraudulent payment chargebacks will result in immediate termination of support privileges and potential revocation of active academic enrollments without restitution.

## 5. Common Questions
- When should I email the Grievance Officer? Only after 48 hours have passed without resolution on your primary in-app ticket.
- Can I call a support phone line at night? Phone support operates from 09:00 IST to 20:00 IST Monday through Saturday.
