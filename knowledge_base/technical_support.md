# Policy: Technical Support and Application Troubleshooting
Last updated: 2026-09-08

## 1. Supported Operating Systems and Browsers
The digital classroom is supported on Android version 8.0 and above, iOS version 15.0 and above, and current evergreen releases of Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, and Microsoft Edge on desktop operating systems. Rooted Android handsets, jailbroken iOS devices, and custom third-party ROMs are strictly blocked by DRM anti-piracy modules.

## 2. Video Player Lag, Buffering, and Resolution Controls
If video playback stutters or exhibits audio de-synchronization during live sessions, students are advised to lower the streaming resolution from 1080p to 480p or 360p in the player settings cog. A steady broadband or cellular connection with at least 5 Mbps continuous download bandwidth is necessary for 720p HD streams without frame drop.

## 3. Application Cache, App Updates, and DRM Errors
Error codes DRM-401 and DRM-504 arise when security certificates expire or screen-recording software is detected running in the background. Students must terminate third-party capture utilities, ensure the application is updated from Google Play Store or Apple App Store, and clear system app storage before relaunching.

## 4. Download Limits and Offline Viewing
Students can download up to 30 lecture sessions per active batch onto their local mobile app sandbox storage for offline consumption. Downloaded files are encrypted, cannot be exported to external SD cards or desktop folders, and expire automatically after 30 days or upon batch conclusion.

## 5. Common Questions
- Why do I see a black screen when lecture starts? Disable screen recorders, virtual machine monitors, or secondary HDMI displays connected to the workstation.
- Can I watch videos on a smart TV via Chromecast? Screencasting is disabled due to content copyright restrictions.
