# Policy: Batch Access and Enrollment Activation
Last updated: 2026-09-12

## 1. Activation Timelines After Purchase
Upon receiving successful payment confirmation with an allocated Order ID, access to the corresponding digital batch is provisioned within 15 to 30 minutes in the "My Batches" portal section. In high-traffic periods such as national examination launch days, batch sync may encounter a queue delay not exceeding 4 hours.

## 2. Inactive Batch Displays and Profile Refresh
If an enrolled course displays as locked or grayed out despite a confirmed invoice, students should log out of the mobile app or web portal, clear app storage cache, and log in again using their registered 10-digit mobile number. Enrolling under a secondary phone number will not link credentials across separate student profiles.

## 3. Multiple Batch Enrolments and Bundles
Students enrolled in combo bundles (e.g., JEE Advanced + Test Series + Doubt Engine) receive staggered module unlocks based on the official academic calendar. Sub-modules, specialized crash lectures, and revision test series become active exactly 48 hours prior to the module start date as announced in the batch schedule dashboard.

## 4. Batch Validity and Access Expiry
Batch access terminates automatically on the date specified in the batch catalog—normally 24 hours after the conclusion of the targeted competitive examination cycle (e.g., JEE Advanced or NEET-UG final shift). Lifetime access is not offered on standard batches, and extensions cannot be granted without purchasing a renewal pass.

## 5. Common Questions
- I bought the course 20 minutes ago and it's not visible? Allow up to 4 hours during peak exam windows, or re-login with your primary registered number.
- Can I pause my batch validity for 2 months? No, batch validity runs continuously according to the academic calendar and cannot be frozen.
