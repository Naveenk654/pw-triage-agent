# Policy: Refund Policy
Last updated: 2026-09-15

## 1. Refund Eligibility Window
Students are eligible to request a refund for live batch or course enrollments within 7 calendar days of the initial batch orientation date or purchase date, whichever is later. Any refund request submitted after 23:59 IST on the 7th calendar day will be rejected automatically without exception. Recorded standalone courses have a 3-day refund window from transaction completion.

## 2. Course Consumption Limits
A refund request will only be entertained if the student has consumed less than 15% of the total scheduled course content, lectures, or Daily Practice Problems (DPPs). Accessing downloadable supplementary PDFs or test papers will count toward total consumption metrics. If lecture watch-time exceeds 4 hours across the batch, the refund is immediately void.

## 3. Non-Refundable Items and Fee Deductions
Registration charges, technology platform fees (Rs. 499), printed study material (modules/books dispatched to address), and gateway processing surcharges are non-refundable. If physical study material was dispatched prior to the refund request, an additional Rs. 1,200 will be deducted from the eligible refund amount.

## 4. Processing Timelines and Payment Method
Once approved by the accounts verification desk, eligible refunds are processed back to the original source payment instrument (UPI, Netbanking, Credit/Debit card) within 7 to 10 working days. No cash refunds, store credit transfers, or payouts to third-party bank accounts are permitted under any circumstance.

## 5. Offline / Vidyapeeth Refund Rules
Offline center (Vidyapeeth/Pathshala) enrollments have separate refund terms. Registration/seat booking fees (typically Rs. 5,000) are non-refundable under all circumstances. If a refund request is submitted before commencement of classes, a partial tuition refund (excluding GST and proportional fees) may be processed. Requests made after the first 7–10 days of batch start are not entertained.

## 6. GMC Scholarship Refund Exception
Students enrolled in designated NEET Dropper programs are eligible for a full tuition fee refund upon securing admission into a Government Medical College (GMC). Verified admission documents (allotment letter + fee receipt from the GMC) must be submitted to the PW accounts team within 30 days of admission confirmation.

## 7. Important Clarification — Online Batch Refunds
Online batches are generally considered non-refundable as digital content access is provided immediately upon purchase. Exceptions are processed only for duplicate payments (technical glitch) and confirmed non-activation after successful payment.

## 8. Common Questions
- Can I get a full refund after 10 days? No, the strict limit is 7 days from purchase or orientation.
- What if I only watched 2 lectures? If total watch-time is under 4 hours and consumption is under 15%, you remain eligible.
- How long does it take for money to reflect? Typically 7-10 business days depending on issuing bank.
