# Policy: Subscription Cancellation and Recurring Billing
Last updated: 2026-09-05

## 1. Monthly and Quarterly Recurring Memberships
Students subscribed to recurring monthly or quarterly self-paced learning plans (such as PW Infinity or PW Pro passes) can initiate subscription cancellation at any time directly through the "Account Settings > Subscriptions" page. To avoid the subsequent renewal cycle billing, the cancellation request must be logged at least 48 hours prior to the next scheduled debit date.

## 2. Access Continuation Post-Cancellation
Initiating a cancellation terminates future automated recurring charges only. The student will retain full active access to all enrolled video archives, doubt channels, and digital test materials until the final day of the already-paid billing period. Once the paid cycle terminates, access converts to standard free public content.

## 3. Discontinuation Does Not Constitute a Refund
Canceling an ongoing recurring subscription stops future billing but does not trigger a retrospective refund for the current or previous billing periods. Fees already collected for an ongoing period are deemed earned and non-refundable, irrespective of the student's actual usage or login frequency during that billing month.

## 4. Failed Renewals and Grace Period
If an automated debit charge fails on the renewal anniversary due to insufficient bank funds or expired card tokens, our billing system executes retry attempts on day 2 and day 4. A 72-hour grace period allows uninterrupted study; after 72 hours of persistent charge failure, the subscription is revoked.

## 5. Common Questions
- If I cancel my subscription on day 5 of the month, do I lose access right away? No, you retain access until the 30th day of the billing period.
- Do I get half my money back if I cancel midway? No, cancellation prevents future debits but does not provide pro-rated refunds.
- Can I cancel my annual one-time batch here? No, one-time full batch enrollments are one-off purchases subject to the 7-day refund policy, not recurring subscription cancellations.
