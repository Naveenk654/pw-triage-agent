# Scholarship and NSAT Policy

## 1. PWNSAT Overview
The PhysicsWallah National Scholarship cum Admission Test (PWNSAT) is our primary scholarship pathway for students in classes 5–12 and droppers. It is conducted in both online and offline modes to provide financial assistance to deserving students.

## 2. Scholarship Tiers
Students can earn up to 100% scholarships on tuition fees based on their PWNSAT score percentile. The scholarship percentage is determined solely by the student's rank and performance in the test. 

## 3. Merit-Based Board Discounts
In addition to PWNSAT, we offer merit-based discounts for board exam toppers. Students who score 95% or above in their CBSE or State board Class 10/12 exams are eligible for additional fee waivers on our courses.

## 4. How to Apply
To apply for scholarships:
1. Open the PW App or visit pw.live.
2. Navigate to the "Scholarship" section.
3. Register for the upcoming PWNSAT exam.
4. Appear for the test in your preferred mode (online/offline).
Results are typically declared within 7 days of the exam date.

## 5. What's Covered
Scholarships apply strictly to the **tuition fee** component of the course. Registration fees, study material charges, and hostel fees (for Vidyapeeth) are NOT covered under any scholarship and must be paid in full.

## FAQ
**Q: Can I apply for PWNSAT after joining a batch?**
A: No, scholarships must be applied before enrolling in a batch. Retrospective discounts are not provided.

**Q: Is my scholarship transferable to another batch?**
A: Scholarships are generally batch-specific. If you transfer your course, the scholarship amount may need to be recalculated based on the new batch's fee structure.
