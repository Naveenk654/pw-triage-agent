# Policy: Course and Batch Transfer Policy
Last updated: 2026-09-14

## 1. Same-Student Internal Batch Switching
A registered student may apply to change from their currently enrolled course batch to another comparable batch of equivalent target exam level (for instance, switching from Lakshya JEE 2027 Hindi medium to Lakshya JEE 2027 English medium) within 14 calendar days from the date of initial orientation. Internal batch changes are allowed at most once per academic year for an individual student account.

## 2. Price Differences and Processing Fees
If the target batch carries a higher retail fee than the original course, the student must remit the exact difference amount before the migration is executed. If migrating to a course with a lower fee structure, the surplus balance is not refunded or returned in cash; rather, it is credited as non-withdrawable wallet points valid for test series addons. A flat administrative processing surcharge of Rs. 250 applies to all internal changes.

## 3. Academic Progress and Assignment Resets
When a student switches to an alternative batch, their previous attendance records, test analytics, and submitted DPP answers are archived in the historical ledger. The new batch starts fresh from lecture module 1 in their active view; archived test submissions cannot be carried forward into the new batch's leaderboards.

## 4. Common Questions
- How many times can I switch my batch medium? Only one internal batch switch is permitted per academic session.
- Do my completed test marks transfer to the new batch? No, assignments and tests must be retaken in the newly selected batch.
- Can I upgrade from a Hindi to an English batch after orientation? Yes, within 14 calendar days upon paying fee differences.
