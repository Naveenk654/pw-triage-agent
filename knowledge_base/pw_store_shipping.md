# PW Store and Shipping Policy

## 1. Ordering
Merchandise and books can be ordered directly through the PW Store (store.pw.live). Select your desired items, add them to your cart, and proceed to checkout.

## 2. Shipping Charges
PhysicsWallah offers **free shipping** on all orders above ₹500. For orders below this amount, standard shipping charges apply at checkout. Orders are typically dispatched the next working day.

## 3. Delivery Timeline
- **Major Cities:** Delivery usually takes **3–5 working days** from the date of dispatch.
- **Remote Locations:** Delivery may take up to **15–20 days** due to logistical constraints.

## 4. Logistics Partners and Tracking
We partner with top logistics providers including Delhivery, Xpressbees, Shiprocket, and Indian Post. 
Once dispatched, a tracking number is sent via email and notification. You can track your order directly on store.pw.live under the "Orders" section.

## 5. Damaged or Missing Items
If you receive damaged goods or if items are missing from your package, please contact support@pw.live with your Order ID and clear photos of the package and items within **48 hours of delivery**.

## 6. Returns
Physical books and study materials are not returnable once opened. However, defective or damaged items reported within the 48-hour window will be replaced free of charge.

## FAQ
**Q: Where is my order?**
A: You can track your order status in the PW App or website under the "Orders" section using your tracking number.

**Q: Book pages are missing, what do I do?**
A: Please email support@pw.live within 48 hours of receiving the book with photos of the missing/blank pages for a replacement.
