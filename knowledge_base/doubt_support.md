# Policy: Academic Doubt Resolution Support
Last updated: 2026-09-06

## 1. Doubt Submission Channels and Daily Quotas
Students registered in eligible live classroom batches possess access to the in-app Doubt Engine. Students can post up to 10 academic doubts per calendar day across Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Biology modules. Doubts must pertain directly to official course curriculum, DPP problems, or scheduled module test questions.

## 2. Response Time Service Level Agreements (SLAs)
Standard text and audio doubt explanations from certified Teaching Assistants (TAs) are delivered within 6 to 8 operating hours between 08:00 IST and 22:00 IST. Inquiries submitted past 22:00 IST enter the morning resolution queue and will receive responses by 12:00 noon the subsequent business day. Peak exam revision phases may observe SLAs expanding up to 12 hours.

## 3. Scope of Academic Assistance and Out-of-Scope Queries
TAs address questions directly related to prescribed syllabus chapters, homework exercises, and standard competitive reference books. TAs do not solve external school homework projects, college laboratory coursework, unrelated private test papers, or non-academic counseling concerns through the Doubt Engine console.

## 4. Live Lecture Q&A Moderation
During real-time broadcast lectures, chat queries are reviewed by dedicated co-faculty members. Faculty address high-frequency conceptual bottlenecks live on stream. Students indulging in spamming, off-topic chat banter, or inappropriate commentary face 24-hour chat mutes or permanent suspension of live chat interaction rights.

## 5. Ask AI — 24/7 Automated Doubt Resolution
Most live batches include a built-in "Ask AI" feature accessible 24/7 within the PW app. Students can type or photograph their question and receive an instant AI-powered explanation. For complex or conceptual doubts that require human expertise, use the standard Doubt Engine to reach a certified Teaching Assistant.

## 6. Common Questions
- Can I upload a photo of my school physics assignment? TAs only solve queries connected to batch curriculum and DPP question banks.
- Why was my doubt closed without reply? Questions with blurry photos or multiple questions clubbed in a single photo ticket are automatically declined.
