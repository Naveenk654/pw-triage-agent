# App Login and OTP Support

## 1. Login Method
Access to the PhysicsWallah app and website is secured via your registered 10-digit mobile number and a One-Time Password (OTP).

## 2. OTP Not Received
If you do not receive the OTP:
- Check if SMS permissions are enabled for the PW app.
- Ensure your network is active (try switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data).
- Wait for 2 minutes and click "Resend OTP".

## 3. Phone Number Change
To change your registered mobile number, email support@pw.live with your old number, new number, and a valid identity proof.

## 4. Multi-Device Login Limits
For security, an account can be logged in on a maximum of two devices simultaneously. Attempting to log into a third device will automatically log out the first device.

## 5. Account Recovery
If you lose access to your phone number and cannot receive OTPs, email support@pw.live with your registration details (Order ID, payment receipt) for manual account recovery.

## FAQ
**Q: I am stuck on the loading screen after entering OTP.**
A: Clear the app cache from your phone settings and ensure you have a stable internet connection.

**Q: Can I use email to login instead of phone number?**
A: Currently, login is strictly tied to your registered 10-digit mobile number via OTP for security reasons.
