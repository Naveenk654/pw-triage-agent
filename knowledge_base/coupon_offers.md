# Policy: Coupon Codes, Discounts, and Promotional Offers
Last updated: 2026-09-01

## 1. Application and Stacking Rules
Promotional coupon codes released across official promotional campaigns, YouTube live events, or early-bird announcements must be entered in the designated checkout promo box prior to initiating payment. Under no circumstances can coupon discounts be combined, stacked, or clubbed with existing early-bird concessions or referral credits. Exactly one coupon applies per order transaction.

## 2. Expiration and Validity Windows
Every discount voucher comes with a predefined expiration timestamp (usually 23:59 IST on the announced campaign date). Expired vouchers cannot be revived or honored retroactively under any circumstances. If a technical error prevents coupon entry during a live promotion, students must capture a timestamped screenshot and notify the help desk within 2 hours of checkout.

## 3. Post-Purchase Discount Claims
Promotional codes cannot be claimed retrospectively after an order is successfully finalized. If a student finishes purchasing a course without applying an active coupon, our billing system does not process retroactive partial refunds or credit the difference amount. Students may, however, leverage valid coupons on subsequent test package additions.

## 4. Wallet Cashback and Referral Credits
Referral incentive credits gained when a peer completes an enrollment using your student referral code are credited directly to your digital rewards wallet. Reward points can subsidize up to 20% of subsequent course tuition or 50% of test series purchases, but points hold zero cash-out value and expire after 180 days from receipt.

## 5. Common Questions
- I bought the course 10 minutes before a 20% coupon was released. Can I get a partial refund? No, discount codes cannot be applied retroactively to completed payments.
- Can I transfer my referral wallet credits to a bank account? No, referral wallet credits are non-withdrawable and usable only for platform enrollments.
