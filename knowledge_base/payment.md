# Policy: Payment and Transaction Policy
Last updated: 2026-09-10

## 1. Supported Payment Modes and Gateways
Payments for subscription courses, test packages, and physical books must be completed through certified payment partners (Razorpay, Paytm, Cashfree, and UPI rails). All transactions must originate from valid domestic cards, netbanking accounts, or UPI handles registered with NPCI. International credit cards require 3D Secure 2.0 verification.

## 2. Failed Transactions and Debited Amounts
If money is deducted from your bank or UPI account but the batch access is not activated, this indicates a payment reconciliation hold. Gateways perform automatic settlement sweeps within 48 to 72 bank working hours. If the order is marked failed by our system, the amount will reverse to your source account automatically without requiring manual staff intervention.

## 3. Double Deductions and Duplicate Payments
In cases where a student accidentally initiates two payments for the same batch order ID within 15 minutes, our automated ledger marks the second payment as a redundant credit. The duplicate charge will be scheduled for auto-refund within 3 to 5 business days to the initiating card or VPA, while the first payment remains tied to active enrollment.

## 4. EMI and Installment Schedules
Third-party no-cost EMI and Cardless credit are governed by the respective lending partner terms. Missing an EMI installment will prompt our automated portal to suspend batch privileges after a 5-day grace notification period. Re-activation occurs within 6 hours of successful settlement verification.

## 5. Common Questions
- Money was debited but course is not showing in My Batches? Wait 48 hours for gateway settlement before submitting an escalation ticket with your Transaction Reference ID.
- Can I pay using split UPI IDs? No, split transactions across two accounts for a single order are unsupported.
- Is offline cash payment supported? No, cash transactions are not supported.
