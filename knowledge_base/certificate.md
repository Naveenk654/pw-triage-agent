# Policy: Course Completion Certificate Policy
Last updated: 2026-08-28

## 1. Eligibility Criteria for Certification
To qualify for a verified digital Certificate of Course Completion, an enrolled student must fulfill two concurrent criteria: attend or view at least 80% of scheduled core video lectures, and achieve an aggregate score of at least 60% across all cumulative milestone batch assessments and DPP assignments before the official course conclusion date.

## 2. Issuance Timelines and Verification Codes
Eligible students receive an automated notification within 14 business days after course closure. Certificates are generated in cryptographically verifiable PDF format, featuring a unique 12-character alphanumeric authentication code and QR link traceable on our public verification ledger (verify.pw-fictional.edu/cert).

## 3. Name Corrections and Re-Issuance
The name imprinted on the completion certificate is drawn verbatim from the student profile registered in the mobile app. Students may submit a correction request for spelling adjustments within 30 days of certificate issue by supplying matching school or board documentation. A nominal re-issuance fee of Rs. 150 applies for printed physical copy dispatches.

## 4. Hardcopy Certificate Delivery
Digital certificates are issued free of charge to all qualifying learners. Students requiring an embossed hardcopy parchment delivered by express courier can place an order through the student storefront, incurring an Rs. 350 shipping and laminating charge. Delivery transpires across 10 to 14 business days across India.

## 5. Common Questions
- If I watch 75% of lectures, can I get a certificate? No, the 80% attendance threshold is strictly enforced by the automated certification portal.
- How can employers verify my certificate? Employers can enter the 12-character certificate code on our online verification portal.
